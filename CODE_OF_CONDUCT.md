# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in any project space, including issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository (or GitHub's private reporting). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly violate it.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
